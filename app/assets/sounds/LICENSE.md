# Sounds

`dice-1.mp3`, `dice-2.mp3` and `dice-3.mp3` are three of the dice throws from
**Casino Audio (1.1)** by Kenney Vleugels (<https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio>),
released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**,
<http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>: free for personal and
commercial use; credit is not required but is given here.

They are the pack's `dice-throw-1.ogg`, `dice-throw-2.ogg` and `dice-throw-3.ogg`,
converted to mono MP3 (96 kbit/s, so every phone can decode them) and raised to
a common peak level. Nothing else was changed.

Every other sound in the game is synthesised in `src/sound.js`.
