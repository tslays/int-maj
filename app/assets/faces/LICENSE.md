# Tile face licences

The table prints one of three face sets, chosen in Settings, Style, Tile
faces. Each folder holds the 42 faces of one set under the canonical keys
(`b1`-`b9` bamboo, `c1`-`c9` characters, `o1`-`o9` circles, `wE wS wW wN`
winds, `dR dG dW` dragons, `f1`-`f4` flowers, `s1`-`s4` seasons) and a
`LICENSE.md` with its exact sources, licences and modifications.

| Folder | Set | Play tiles | Flowers and seasons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `riichi/` | Riichi (default) | riichi-mahjong-tiles by FluffyStuff, `Regular/`, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Cangjie6, Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), inks recoloured |
| `riichi-dark/` | Riichi Dark | riichi-mahjong-tiles by FluffyStuff, `Black/`, CC0 1.0 | Cangjie6, CC BY-SA 4.0, inks recoloured |
| `chinese/` | Classic Chinese | Cangjie6, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 | the same set, CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Every face is an SVG drawing with no embedded pictures or text. The faces
taken from Cangjie6, as derivatives, stay under CC BY-SA 4.0, and each one
names its source and licence in a comment.
