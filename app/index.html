How to use the app

You play international mahjong against three bots, offline on this device. This sheet is about the app; the rules are in the book.

The table

  • You sit at the bottom, your hand along the lower edge. The bots sit left, across and right.
  • The panel in the centre shows the round and hand (for example East 2), the tiles left in the wall, and every seat's wind and score. The dealer's wind is coral, and a soft light marks whose turn it is.
  • Each seat's discards lie in front of it; the newest one rests on a light plate. Melds and bonus tiles sit beside each wall.
  • Study the discards: tap the middle of the table to see them large, from above. Tap anywhere to go back. It works while the bots play too.

Your turn: discarding

  • Tap a tile to lift it, tap it again to throw it. Or hold it and swipe up, then let go; slide back down to cancel.
  • With a tile lifted, tapping another moves the lift, so you can compare. Tap empty table to put it down.
  • With a mouse, the tile under the pointer lifts by itself and one click throws it.
  • To arrange your hand yourself, drag a tile sideways; it trades places with the tiles it passes. That turns Sort off.

The buttons above your hand

They appear only when the game offers you the choice, with a chime. Draws, and discards you cannot claim, happen by themselves.

  • Pung, Chow: take the discard shown at the left of the bar. With several possible chows, tap Chow, then the run you want (Back returns).
  • Kong: declare a kong from your own tiles.
  • Mah Jong: declare your winning hand, from the wall or on a discard.
  • Fishing: announce that you are one tile from winning; the tiles shown are the ones you wait on. Don't announce keeps it to yourself.
  • Pass: decline the discard.
  • Reshuffle: on your first turn, when the deal gives you nothing to build on, ask for a new deal.
  • Pass 3 tiles: in a Goulash, tap three tiles to pass to the seat named on the banner, then confirm.

Menu

  • New game: start again from the first hand. The game in progress is dropped.
  • Calls: lit, you are asked about every discard you could claim. Off, you are not asked about Pung, Chow or Kong, which cuts the pauses. You are still asked about Mah Jong.
  • Sort: lit, the game keeps your hand in order. After you have rearranged it by hand, tap it to sort again.
  • Hints: show or hide the Closest hands panel.
  • Options: opens a sheet with the Sound switch (on iPhone the silent switch also mutes it), the house rules, Report (send a problem to the developers, below) and Help, this sheet. The small label under the menu is the build you are playing.

A lit button is on. Hints, Sound and the house rules are remembered on this device.

Closest hands

The panel at the top right lists up to three hands your tiles are nearest to: its name, how many tiles you still need and its value. The eye icon says how to play it: a closed eye means concealed only, so calling a discard rules it out; an open eye means exposed melds are fine. It updates as your tiles change. Tap a row for the hand's sheet: what it is, its picture from the book, what it pays and the tiles you still need. Tap the play chip on a sheet ("Concealed only") for what it means, and the pin at its top right to keep the hand in the panel.

All hands

The button above that panel opens a list of every hand in play, even with Hints off. With all hands in play that is every hand in both books. Search by name or tiles ("dragon pairs", "1-9"), or filter by kind, value, concealed or exposed play and book. During a hand each card says how many tiles you are away, and Order can put the nearest first. Tap a card for its sheet. The cross, a tap outside or Escape closes a sheet.

After a hand

The result card names the winning hand and what it scored, or says the hand was drawn. Each seat shows its change in green or red and its new total; tap a seat to see who paid what, and why. Next hand deals again. After the last hand, Final standings ranks the seats, and New game starts over.

House rules

Options has the table's house rules, such as which hands are in play: all of them, only the Companion's, or only the Companion's that come with a sample picture. A change never touches the hand being played: it takes effect with the next hand, for you and for the other players alike.

Saving

The game saves after every move, on this device only, and picks up where you left off, also after a reload. If an update changes the game, the hand starts again with your scores kept.

Install on your phone

Play with the phone on its side. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu, then Add to Home screen. Installed, it runs full screen and offline.

Report a problem

Open Options, tap Report, add a line about what you saw if you like, and tap Send. It goes to the developers with the build, your device and every move of this game, so the hand can be replayed. No name or account. Without a connection, Share it instead sends it as a message.
