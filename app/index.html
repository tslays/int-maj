How to play

Discard

Tap a tile in your hand, then tap it again to throw it. Or hold a tile and swipe up.

Calling and winning

When you can claim a discard, make a kong, go fishing or declare Mah Jong, a button appears above your hand. Tap it, or let it pass.

Hints

The Hints panel lists the hands you are closest to. Tap a row to open that hand's sheet: what it is, what it pays and which tiles you still need.

Menu

  • New game starts a fresh deal.
  • Calls off: you are no longer asked about discards.
  • Sort keeps your hand in order.
  • Hints shows or hides the closest-hands panel.
  • Options holds the sound, the house rules, this help and Report a problem.

House rules

Options has the table's house rules, such as which hands are in play: all of them, only the Companion's, or only the Companion's that come with a sample picture. A change never touches the hand being played: it takes effect with the next hand, for you and for the other players alike.

Saving

The game saves after every move and picks up where you left off when you come back.

Install on your phone

iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu, then Add to Home screen. Play with the phone on its side.

Report a problem

Open Options and tap Report, add a line about what you saw if you like, and send it. It shares the build, your device and every move of this game, so the hand can be replayed.
