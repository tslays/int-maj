# Primary hands — the illustrated catalogue

Extracted from the owner-designated primary scan
(`scans/The Game of Mah Jong Illustrated - trimmed.pdf`) by reading the
renders in `rules/.generated/primary-pages/`. Page numbers are **printed** pages. The
synopsis is printed pp. 60-61; the score summary for the further variations is
printed p. 50; the short list and the two unnumbered hands are printed p. 30.

Coverage: every printed hand, in book order: 1-51 on pp. 34-58, then the two
unnumbered hands on p. 30. The Companion's extra hands are **not** in this
file; they are listed in `rules/AUDIT.md` under "not printed in this book".

## Notation

- `B` bamboo, `C` circles, `O` characters (萬). `1B` is rank 1 bamboo.
- `×2` pair, `×3` pung, `×4` kong; `1B-2B-3B` a chow.
- Winds `E S W N`; dragons `發` (green), `中` (red), `白` (white, usually a
  blank/framed tile).
- "extra" is the fourteenth tile in a hand whose caption says "any tile
  paired"; the illustration's choice is named.
- Score lines are copied exactly as printed, including capitals and the
  concealed/exposed qualification. `(see score-sheet)` is part of the printed
  line, not an editorial note.

## Synopsis and summary cross-checks

The synopsis (pp. 60-61) covers hands 1-35 and the two unnumbered hands; it
says expressly that it does "not include further variations", whose scores are
on p. 50. Where the synopsis and a hand page differ in detail, the hand page's
illustration and caption are the composition source; the differences are
recorded here rather than resolved silently.

- **Hand 1** has no tier in the synopsis; it is scored by the ground score and
  doubles on pp. 24-25.
- **Hand 2** prints `THREE DOUBLES PLUS EXTRAS (see score-sheet)` on p. 34;
  the synopsis abbreviates it to `Purity (Three doubles)`.
- **Hand 11** prints `10 POINTS FOR NON-SCORING HAND` on p. 38; the synopsis
  prints `Concealed Mah Jong (Buried Treasure) (2 doubles and extras)`.
- **Hand 24**: the hand page says "6 Pairs of green Bamboos"; the synopsis says
  "All Pairs of Green Bamboo ... At least one Pair of Green Dragons" and adds
  "(2 Pairs do not count as a Kong.)" The illustration is six green-bamboo
  pairs (4 Bamboo printed twice) plus one Green Dragon pair.
- **Hand 25**: the synopsis abbreviates to "Pung or Kong of Green Bamboo (2,3,
  4,6,8). Pung of Green Dragons. One Chow, if desired"; the hand page requires
  three green-bamboo sets and a pair.
- **Hands 34 and 35**: the synopsis repeats the page's extra rule that a limit
  is scored for any hand containing pungs of all Dragons, or pungs of all
  Winds, respectively.
- **Hand 36**: the p. 50 summary lists `Dragon's Tail` under Limit only; the
  hand page prints Limit (if concealed) / Half Limit (if exposed).
- **Hands 40-43**: p. 50 lists them under Limit only; their pages print Half
  Limit when the Pungs come from discards.
- **Hand 8**: the illustration literally repeats rank 2 and omits ranks 3 and
  6. The caption does not require a consecutive run, so the composition is
  seven matching-number pairs, not a 1-7 run.
- **Hand 47**: the illustration uses `發`, `中`, `S`, `W`, `N`; the caption says
  only "Five odd Honours, Winds and Dragons", i.e. any five different honour
  tiles.

## Unreadable or uncertain

No page was illegible. The following are printed ambiguities, marked rather
than guessed:

1. **Hand 8**'s repeated rank 2 and missing 3/6 are literal; no correction is
   made.
2. **All Kong** says "all in one suit" while allowing "Winds, Dragons or both";
   read as the suited tiles being one suit and honours allowed, but the page
   does not resolve the wording.
3. **Hand 24**'s "At least one Pair of Green Dragons" with "6 Pairs of green
   Bamboos" forces exactly one dragon pair in a seven-pair hand; the synopsis's
   "at least one" is looser, but the illustration is 6 + 1.
4. **Hand 25**'s "One Chow allowed" is illustrated with three pungs; only the
   caption states the chow option.
5. **Hand 11**'s two score lines differ (`10 POINTS FOR NON-SCORING HAND` on
   p. 38; `2 doubles and extras` in the synopsis); both are recorded.
